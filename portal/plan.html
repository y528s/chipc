One portal for City & Hackney primary care

The GP clinical site and the IPC members area become one site: one login, one search, one WordPress back end. Access is controlled per item, not per area — member content simply carries a 🔒 wherever it appears.

What changes

  • Two sites and two back ends become one portal, one back end
  • One search across pathways, services, documents, contracts and policies
  • Member content marked 🔒 per item — no separate "members area" to navigate
  • Document library rebuilt: in-browser viewing, owners, versions, review dates
  • Editors can make quick edits from the page itself, not just the back end

What stays the same

  • The bulletin — content and weekly workflow untouched, only new chrome around it
  • All existing content — migrated, not rewritten
  • Member logins — same accounts, used in one place instead of two
  • Who owns what — contract leads and policy owners carry over

How editing works

  • One WordPress admin with a single content menu for the whole portal
  • A "Members only" checkbox on any item adds the 🔒 automatically everywhere it appears and gates access
  • Roles decide who edits what: clinical editors handle GP content, contract leads their own contract, IPC editors everything
  • Policy pages carry version, owner and review date — the dashboard flags reviews due

Rollout

Build on staging

New portal built alongside the live sites — nothing changes for users while we build.

Migrate & tag content

Existing pages and documents moved across; IPC items tagged members-only so the 🔒 appears automatically.

Member practice preview

A small group of practices tests search, the document library and logins on staging; feedback folded in.

Switch over

Old addresses redirect to the portal. Both old sites stay frozen and readable for a safety period, then retire.
