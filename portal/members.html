How access works

The hub is one site. Clinical content is open to all practice staff; anything marked 🔒 needs an IPC member login. Members see everything seamlessly — visitors who aren't signed in are asked to log in when they open a locked item.

One back end too: IPC admins manage open and members-only content from the same place — a tick-box marks any page or document as members-only, and the padlock appears automatically everywhere it's listed.
